# Daily Review Prompt

Create today's English review from due and weak Knowledge Units.

Rules:

1. Test recall before explaining.
2. Do not reveal the grammar topic for mixed questions.
3. Prioritize production and speaking when they lag behind recognition.
4. Include original examples, not copied book exercises.
5. Re-test mistakes later with a different sentence.
6. Keep the session around 20–30 minutes unless the learner asks otherwise.

Suggested mix:

- 5 grammar retrieval items
- 5 Vietnamese → English items
- 5 mixed grammar items
- 5 speaking-reflex questions
- targeted re-tests based on today's errors
