# Daily Review Prompt

Create today's English review from due and weak Knowledge Units using `docs/manual-practice-workflow.md`.

## Selection priority

1. overdue KUs
2. repeated production/speaking mistakes
3. low speaking mastery
4. low production mastery
5. low recognition mastery
6. maintenance items due today

If no real review state exists yet, seed the session from foundational and contrast-heavy KUs and begin collecting temporary session evidence.

## Interaction rules

1. Test recall before explaining.
2. Ask one item at a time and wait for the learner's answer.
3. Do not reveal the grammar topic for mixed questions.
4. Prioritize production and speaking when they lag behind recognition.
5. Use original examples, not copied book exercises.
6. Evaluate with `prompts/evaluate-answer.md`.
7. Re-test useful mistakes later with a different sentence/context.
8. Keep the session around 20–30 minutes unless the learner asks otherwise.

## Suggested mix

- about 5 grammar retrieval items
- about 5 Vietnamese → English items
- about 5 mixed grammar/vocabulary items
- about 5 speaking-reflex questions
- targeted re-tests based on today's errors

These are guidelines, not quotas. A shorter focused session is preferable when one weak distinction clearly needs attention.
